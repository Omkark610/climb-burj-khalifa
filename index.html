SKYCLIMBBURJ KHALIFA · 828 m

Scale the outside of the world’s tallest tower at dusk above Downtown Dubai, Burj Lake, and the Arabian Gulf. Stand up and clear a 2 m area.

  • Grip on a glowing hold, then pull down to climb the glass & steel bundled-tube wings
  • Release while moving your hands to launch yourself upward
  • Thumbstick to run on a ledge · A / X / B / Y to jump
  • Checkpoints — Multiple checkpoints are placed along the tower ledges. Collect items at intersections! You have 6 lives to restart from your last checkpoint if you fall.
